How it works

Three steps to a better wardrobe

No stylists, no subscriptions, no uploading your photos to a server. Everything runs on your phone.

01

Tell us about you

Choose women's or men's clothing and optionally add a full-body photo. The photo is processed entirely on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

02

AI ranks every outfit

Pick a style — casual, formal, sporty and more. A neural network trained on tens of thousands of real looks ranks each combination by how well it suits you, not just whether the items match each other.

03

Shop or build

Get ranked, complete outfits from store catalogs, from your own wardrobe, or built around a single item. Tap through to the retailer to buy.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely on your phone (via ONNX Runtime). Your photos are stored only on your device and are never transmitted. See the privacy policy.

When is the app available?

SuitUp is in final testing for Android, ahead of a Google Play release. Email us to join the beta.

How does SuitUp make money?

The app is free. When you buy an item through a link in the app or on this site, the retailer may pay us a small commission through affiliate networks. This never affects the price you pay, and it never affects the AI's scoring — outfits are ranked purely by compatibility. See our affiliate disclosure.

Which regions are supported?

v1 supports catalogs for the United States, Europe, and Israel, in English. SuitUp picks yours from your phone's approximate location — so you see that region's stores, priced in your currency — and tells you if you're somewhere it doesn't cover yet. More regions are planned.

Transparency

Affiliate disclosure

SuitUp participates in affiliate marketing programs. Some product links in the SuitUp app and on this website are affiliate links: if you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you. Product recommendations and outfit rankings are generated by our AI model based solely on style compatibility — commission rates play no role in what we recommend or how outfits are ranked.
